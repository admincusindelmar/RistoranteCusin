# Le castagne col pesce · testi per generare la foto con l'IA

Scritti in inglese, perché i programmi di immagini lo capiscono meglio. Si usano senza caricare foto. Formato consigliato: orizzontale, 5:2 per la mail (1200×480) oppure 3:2 per i social. Da presentare come foto d'atmosfera.

## Prompt 1 · Risotto alla ricciola, cachi e castagne

Professional food photography, close-up of an elegant fine-dining risotto served in a wide white porcelain bowl with a textured, pearl-dotted rim. Creamy risotto topped with thin raw slices of amberjack fish, small cubes of ripe orange persimmon, crumbled roasted chestnuts and a thin drizzle of dark amber chestnut honey; a few micro herbs. Shot at table level, shallow depth of field, the plate sharp in the foreground. Background softly blurred: an elegant seaside restaurant terrace with white tablecloths, beige chairs and small candle lights, the sea at sunset with a pink and orange sky. Warm, natural light, realistic textures, no text, no people, no logos.
